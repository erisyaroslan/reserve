Why ReServe?

Affordable Meals

Get quality meals at lower prices, making it easier for students to enjoy nutritious food without straining their limited budgets. By offering discounted surplus meals, ReServe helps students manage daily expenses while still eating well.

Reduce Food Waste

Help vendors sell surplus food instead of throwing it away, addressing food waste challenges within the campus community. ReServe enables vendors to efficiently redistribute excess meals while promoting responsible consumption.

Campus Focused

Designed for university students and nearby food vendors, ensuring the platform meets the specific needs of the campus community. ReServe creates a convenient and trusted space for local food exchange within a close and familiar environment.
