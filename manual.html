The ReServe Journey

A smarter way to eat, a simpler way to save. Follow the path from campus surplus to your next sustainable meal.

Step 01

Vendors List Surplus

Local vendors identify meals that are perfectly good but unsold. They upload them with a significant discount, ensuring nothing goes to waste.

Step 02

Claim Your Meal

Browse the live map in real-time. Once you find a meal you like, reserve it instantly. No more wondering what's left at the end of the day.

Step 03

Quick Pickup

Head to the location, show your digital receipt, and grab your food. Fast, easy, and you just helped save a perfectly good meal from the bin.
